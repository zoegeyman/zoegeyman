A little more

The longer version

I trained as a cultural anthropologist, which means my job has always been to understand people on their own terms. At the American Institutes for Research, I moderated focus groups, wrote interview protocols, and turned technical findings into plain language deliverables for federal clients.

For my Ph.D. at Washington University in St. Louis, I spent 12 months in Delhi studying how consumption and taste shape the way NGO employees think about status and success. That project, supported by a Fulbright-Hays award, taught me how to earn trust with people who are wary of researchers, and how much the small choices people make (what they eat, wear and buy) can tell you.

I'm looking for consumer insights roles where close listening turns into better decisions.
